Recent work

A knob that hadn’t been made in 100 years

Jaime was restoring a 1920 Chevy and needed the reverse light knob. Not reproduced, not reproduced well, and the one original he found cost him $130. He sent a photo of that original from his phone.

I modeled it from the photo and his caliper measurements, then ran a batch of ten so the rest of his club could stop hunting for theirs.

Why send it to me

A real engineer, not a hobby printer

CSWA-certified in SolidWorks, with a manufacturing background. That means parts designed to fit and hold, with tolerances that account for how plastic actually behaves — not a shape traced in a free browser tool.

You talk to me

No ticket system, no upload portal, no support queue. You text a photo and a person who knows the answer texts back. If your part is a bad candidate for printing, I’ll tell you that instead of taking the order.

No job is too small

One knob. One clip. One weird bracket for one specific machine. The big platforms can’t price that job and won’t take it. It’s the job I built the shop around.

Honest timelines

Most parts ship in 3 to 7 days. If yours is going to take longer — a tricky geometry, a finish that needs curing time — you hear that up front, before you pay.
